## Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Serving pages involves processing technical request
information such as your IP address. GitHub explains its logging, retention,
international transfers, and privacy rights in its
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Audience statistics

This website uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to understand which
pages receive visits and where visitors arrive from. Google Analytics is not used.
GoatCounter does not place analytics cookies or store tracking information in your
browser's local storage.

GoatCounter documents aggregate reports of page views and referring sites, with
optional statistics about location, browser, language, and device. To distinguish
repeat visits, it temporarily processes the site name, IP address, and browser
user-agent in memory for up to eight hours. These raw identifiers are not stored
in its analytics database. Individual-pageview recording is an optional setting,
disabled by default.

The hosted service stores data in Finland and Germany. See
[GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for its
processing and deletion practices.

## Email and subscriptions

If you email me, I receive your address and the information you choose to send so
I can respond.

The subscription link opens your email application to contact **rssby.email**.
Submitting that email is your choice; this website does not itself collect a
subscriber email list. The service says it stores your address until you
unsubscribe. Its [privacy and unsubscribe information](https://rssby.email/)
explains how to stop receiving updates. You can also follow the
[RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}) using your own feed reader.
