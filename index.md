I work in theoretical computer science, mainly in algebraic complexity theory. I am interested in understanding what makes computational problems hard, especially when algebra is involved. My interests also extend to graph theory, algorithms, computational number theory, and group testing.

I am currently a DAAD PRIME Fellow at both Universität des Saarlandes and Université Savoie Mont Blanc, working with [Prof. Markus Bläser](https://cc.cs.uni-saarland.de/mblaeser/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and [Dr. Sébastien Tavenas](https://tavenas.pages.math.cnrs.fr/home.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, respectively. Before this, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the IT University of Copenhagen and BARC, working with Prof. Nutan Limaye. I completed my PhD at IIT Kanpur under the supervision of Prof. Nitin Saxena.

Outside research, I spend a lot of time reading. I do not stick to one genre, but I particularly enjoy historical fiction, biographies, and fantacy novels. I also enjoy talking about books, often just as much as reading them.

If you would like to talk about research, books, or something else we might have in common, feel free to get in touch.
